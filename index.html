The Story

What Happens When Life Covers Who You Are?

Joseph Bennett has spent much of his life concerned about other people and deeply aware of how his decisions might affect them. He offers grace and understanding to those around him while struggling to extend the same patience and consideration to himself.

Through loss, divorce, difficult relationships, guilt, trauma, and unexpected changes, Joseph begins to question how much of his life has been shaped by circumstances he could not control.

There are many things we cannot choose, but there are still choices that remain ours.

Beneath Everything is a story about discovering what remains when life does not unfold as expected and learning that a difficult past does not have to become the only explanation for a person's future.

Meet Joseph

The Person Before the Story

Joseph Bennett is not a hero who has everything figured out. He is a man learning how to live while carrying experiences that have changed him.

He cares deeply about other people, sometimes to the point of carrying concerns that were never his responsibility. He worries about consequences that may never happen and often finds it easier to offer grace to others than to give that same grace to himself.

Joseph's journey is not about becoming a person untouched by his past. It is about learning that his past can influence him without possessing the authority to determine everything that comes next.

What happens when a person finally gives themselves the grace they have always given everyone else?

The Central Message

Bury Your Fear.
Reveal Your Talent.

Burying fear does not mean pretending that fear does not exist. It means refusing to allow fear to become the authority behind every decision.

Revealing talent means allowing the abilities, creativity, ideas, and potential within a person to receive attention and development rather than remaining hidden because uncertainty makes beginning difficult.

What might still be waiting to be revealed?

Behind the Story

Inspired by Real Conversations

Beneath Everything is a work of narrative storytelling inspired in part by personal conversations and experiences shared with the author with permission.

Joseph Bennett's story has been developed as a literary narrative intended to encourage reflection, understanding, grace, and personal consideration while respecting the privacy and dignity of the experiences that contributed to its creation.

About the Author

Bradley Hobbs

Bradley Hobbs is the author of Beneath Everything: The Story of Joseph Bennett.

Through narrative storytelling, Bradley explores the experiences that shape the way people understand themselves, their relationships, their past, and the possibilities that may still remain ahead of them.

Beneath Everything invites readers to consider that difficult experiences can become part of a person's story without becoming the only explanation for who they are or who they may still become.
