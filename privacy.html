Privacy and Personal Information Notice

Beneath Everything: Bury Your Fear and Reveal Your Talent respects the privacy and personal dignity of individuals whose experiences and conversations contributed to the development of this narrative.

The story of Joseph Bennett was developed in part from personal conversations and experiences shared with the author with permission to use those experiences as inspiration for this work. The author has made reasonable efforts to present the narrative respectfully while protecting personal privacy.

This book is not intended to serve as a complete biography, factual record, clinical case study, or detailed account of any individual's private life. Personal details may have been omitted, adapted, combined, reorganized, or presented differently for literary and narrative purposes.

The publication of this book does not grant readers permission to investigate, identify, contact, harass, or publicly speculate about any person who may have contributed experiences or conversations that inspired the narrative.

No private personal information is intentionally included for the purpose of identifying, exposing, or causing harm to any individual.

Any website, newsletter, online store, contact form, or other digital service connected to the author or this book may have its own separate Privacy Policy governing the collection and use of visitor or customer information.

The purpose of this narrative is to encourage reflection and understanding while respecting the personal experiences that contributed to its development.
