# Beneath Everything | The Story of Joseph Bennett

A Christian teaching and writing project inviting readers to look beneath outward appearances and examine the deeper spiritual realities of the heart, faith, and daily life.

## Live Website
https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/beneath-everything/

## Project Overview
The project's main reading pages, chapters, and supporting materials available in the site.

Homepage title: **Beneath Everything | The Story of Joseph Bennett**. Beneath Everything: The Story of Joseph Bennett is a narrative story by Bradley Hobbs about loss, grace, personal choice, and revealing the talents that remain within us.
