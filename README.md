## About the Project

This repository contains the official landing page for **Beneath Everything: The Story of Joseph Bennett**, a narrative book by **Bradley Hobbs**.

The website is designed to introduce readers to Joseph Bennett and the central message of the story while creating a cinematic and reflective experience that reflects the tone of the book.

The story explores experiences that can shape a person's life, including fear, trauma, loss, guilt, relationships, and the struggle to offer ourselves the same grace we often give to others.

At the center of Joseph's journey is a simple but meaningful realization:

> Fear does not have to decide everything that remains possible.

---

# The Story

Joseph Bennett is a man who has spent much of his life concerned about other people and deeply aware of how his decisions might affect them. He often extends grace and understanding to others while struggling to offer the same patience and consideration to himself.

Through experiences involving loss, divorce, difficult relationships, guilt, trauma, and unexpected changes, Joseph begins to question how much of his life has been shaped by circumstances he could not control.

His journey eventually brings him to an important understanding:

> There are many things we cannot choose, but there are still choices that remain ours.

**Beneath Everything** is a story about discovering what remains when life does not unfold as expected and learning that a difficult past does not have to become the only explanation for a person's future.

---

# Bury Your Fear and Reveal Your Talent

The phrase **"Bury Your Fear and Reveal Your Talent"** is central to the story.

Burying fear does not mean pretending fear does not exist, suppressing difficult emotions, or denying the experiences that have affected us. Instead, it means refusing to allow fear to become the authority behind every decision.

Revealing talent is about allowing the abilities, ideas, creativity, and potential within a person to receive attention and development rather than remaining hidden because uncertainty makes beginning difficult.

The story asks readers to consider an important question:

**What might still be waiting to be revealed?**

# Book Structure

## Introduction

### What We Bury

An introduction to the experiences that can cause people to hide parts of themselves and the possibility that something meaningful may still remain.

---

## Chapter One

### The Person Before the Story

Joseph Bennett's internal world and the life he carried before fully understanding how much of himself had been affected by his experiences.

---

## Chapter Two

### The Battle That Was Not His

Joseph begins recognizing that caring deeply about other people does not mean accepting responsibility for every problem around him.

---

## Chapter Three

### The Things He Could Not Hold Together

Joseph experiences divorce, loss, and the difficult realization that some circumstances cannot be controlled simply by worrying about them.

---

## Chapter Four

### The Life He Did Not Expect

An unexpected relationship challenges Joseph's assumptions about fear, relationships, and the future.

---

## Chapter Five

### Beneath Everything Is My Choice

Joseph begins to understand that his past can influence him without possessing the authority to determine everything that comes next.

---

## Epilogue

### What Remained

Joseph does not arrive at the end of his journey with every question answered, but he begins to understand that moving forward does not require becoming untouched by the past.
